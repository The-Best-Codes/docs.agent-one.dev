---
title: Text-to-Speech
description: Have AgentOne read its responses out loud with OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Hume, or Google Gemini voices.
---

AgentOne can read its responses out loud. Once you set up a voice provider, a **Read aloud** button appears under every response.

## Set Up a Voice

Go to `Settings` -> `Providers` -> **Text-to-Speech**.

![The Text-to-Speech card with a provider picker.](/images/docs/models/text-to-speech.png)

Choose a **Provider**, pick a **Voice Model**, paste the provider's **API Key**, then choose a voice and adjust the options.

| Provider          | Voices                                                                                 | Options                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OpenAI**        | alloy, ash, ballad, coral, echo, fable, nova, onyx, sage, shimmer, verse, marin, cedar | **Speech Rate**, and **How It Should Sound** for a speaking style                                                               |
| **ElevenLabs**    | Paste any voice ID from your ElevenLabs account                                        | **Speech Rate**, **Language Code**, **Stability**, **Voice Match**, **Style Strength**, **Text Cleanup**, **Boost Voice Match** |
| **Hume**          | Paste a Hume voice ID                                                                  | **Speech Rate**, **How It Should Sound**                                                                                        |
| **Google Gemini** | Choose from Gemini's voice list                                                        | **Speech Rate**, **How It Should Sound**                                                                                        |

**How It Should Sound** lets you describe the delivery in plain words, like "upbeat and friendly" or "calm and slow."

## Read a Response Aloud

Click **Read aloud** under any response. Click the same button again (**Stop reading aloud**) to stop.

Your voice settings sync across your devices when you're signed in, but the API key stays on each computer.
