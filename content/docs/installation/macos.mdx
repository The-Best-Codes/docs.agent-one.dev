## Install AgentOne on macOS

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Steps>
<Step>
  
    ### Download the `dmg` file
    
    Click the `Download app` button in the bottom left of your [account dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) or head to:
    https://www.agent-one.dev/download
    
    ![AgentOne sidebar showing Download app button](/images/docs/installation/windows/sidebar-download-app.png)
    
    You'll be redirected to a download page, which will attempt to automatically detect your operating system. Make sure the `macOS` tab at the top of the page is selected:
    
    ![AgentOne download page showing macOS tab](/images/docs/installation/macos/download-macos-tab.png)
    
    You'll see two download options:
    
    - **Download Apple Silicon (.dmg):** for Macs with Apple Silicon chips (M1, M2, M3, M4, or newer).
    - **Download Intel (.dmg):** for older Macs with Intel processors.
    
    Click the button that matches your Mac.
    
    <Callout title="Not sure which one to pick?">
      Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen and select `About This Mac`. Look at the `Chip` (or `Processor`) field:
      
      - If it says something like `Apple M1`, `Apple M2`, `Apple M3`, `Apple M4`, or `Apple M5`, choose **Apple Silicon**.
      - If it says something like `Intel Core i5` or `Intel Core i7`, choose **Intel**.
      
      If you pick the wrong one, AgentOne may not run correctly or may run more slowly than it should. To learn more, read [Apple's support article.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943)
    </Callout>
  
</Step>
<Step>
  
    ### Run the installer
    
    You're almost done! After downloading the installer, open it (click or double-click the `.dmg` file) and follow the prompts to install AgentOne on your system.
  
</Step>
</Steps>
