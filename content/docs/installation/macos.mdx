## Install AgentOne on macOS

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Steps>
<Step>
  
    ### Download the `dmg` file
    
    Click the `Download app` button in the bottom left of your [account dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) or head to:
    https://www.agent-one.dev/download
    
    ![AgentOne sidebar showing Download app button](/images/docs/installation/windows/sidebar-download-app.png)
    
    You'll be redirected to a download page, which will attempt to automatically detect your operating system. Make sure the `macOS` tab at the top of the page is selected:
    
    ![AgentOne download page showing Windows tab](/images/docs/installation/macos/download-macos-tab.png)
    
    Now click `Download`.
  
</Step>
<Step>
  
    ### Run the installer
    
    You're almost done! After downloading the installer, open it (click or double-click the `.dmg` file) and follow the prompts to install AgentOne on your system.
  
</Step>
</Steps>
