Get the desktop app from the [AgentOne download page](https://www.agent-one.dev/download). It detects your operating system, but you can select another tab if you need to.

## Pick the correct build

Choose the operating system and CPU architecture that match your computer. On a Mac, **About This Mac** shows whether you have an Apple chip or an Intel processor. On Linux, `uname -m` helps identify your architecture; `x86_64` corresponds to amd64/x64, while `aarch64` corresponds to ARM64.

Available packages can differ between releases. If a format or architecture is missing from the website, check the assets on the [official release page](https://github.com/AgentOne-Dev/agent-one-public/releases/latest). Use the stable release unless you intentionally want to test a prerelease.

## After installation

Open AgentOne and follow [Your first chat](/docs/getting-started/first-chat). The model and extension catalogs download separately when needed, so an installed app may still need an internet connection to populate those lists.

For updates, use your installation source and [Help & Updates](/docs/settings/updates). Store and community packages can arrive at a different time from the direct desktop release.

## Reinstalling or changing computers

Export important chats before uninstalling, replacing an installation, or removing application data. An installer isn't a backup, and [settings sync](/docs/account/data-and-sync) doesn't copy your whole workspace. Configure providers and extensions on the new device before relying on them.

The application source is available in [The-Best-Codes/agent-one](https://github.com/The-Best-Codes/agent-one). Downloadable releases are also distributed through [AgentOne-Dev/agent-one-public](https://github.com/AgentOne-Dev/agent-one-public/releases/latest); the two repositories serve different purposes.
