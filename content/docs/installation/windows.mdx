There are two primary ways to install AgentOne on Windows.

## Install from agent-one.dev (recommended)

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Steps>
<Step>
      
      ### Sign in to your AgentOne account
      
      Navigate to https://www.agent-one.dev/sign-in in your browser to sign in. If you don't have an account, [sign up instead](https://www.agent-one.dev/sign-up).
      
</Step>
<Step>
  
    ### Navigate to [your dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard)
  
</Step>
<Step>
  
    ### Download the desktop app installer
    
    Click the "Download app" button in the bottom left of the sidebar.
    
    {/* TOFILL: Image here */}
    
    You'll be redirected to a download page, which will attempt to automatically detect your operating system. Make sure the "Windows" tab at the top of the page is selected:
    
    {/* TOFILL: Image here */}
    
    Now click "Download AgentOne for Windows".
  
</Step>
<Step>
  
    ### Run the installer
    
    You're almost done! After downloading the installer, open it (click or double-click the `.exe` file) and follow the prompts to install AgentOne on your system. If you get stuck, refer to the [Windows Installer](#windows-installer) section below.
  
</Step>
</Steps>
