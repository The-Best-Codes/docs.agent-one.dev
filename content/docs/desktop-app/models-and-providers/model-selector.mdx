The model selector sits at the bottom of the chat composer.

## How It Works

- it shows the currently selected provider and model
- clicking it opens a searchable model list
- models are grouped by provider

## Search Inside The Selector

Use the built-in search field to quickly filter models by name or ID.

## What Appears In The Selector

Only enabled and available chat models appear here.

That means a model will not show up if:

- its provider is disabled
- the model has not been added to a custom provider
- the local provider is not reachable and has no configured models
- AgentOne models are hidden in account settings

## Mobile-Sized Layout

On narrower windows, the selector opens in a drawer instead of a popover.
