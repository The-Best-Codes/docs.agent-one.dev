AgentOne separates providers from models.

- A provider is the service or runtime that exposes models
- A model is the specific option you choose when chatting

## Provider Types

AgentOne supports three provider groups in the app:

- `Built-in Providers`
- `Local Providers`
- `Custom Providers`
