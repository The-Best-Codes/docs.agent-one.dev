The first time you open AgentOne, you will go through a short onboarding flow.

## Step 1: Splash Screen

The app opens with a splash screen and a `Get Started` button. Sick animation, huh?

{/* Screenshot placeholder: onboarding splash screen with the initial Get Started button. */}

## Step 2: Choose Your Name

AgentOne asks `What should I call you?`

Enter the name you want AgentOne to use when addressing you. This can be changed later in `Settings` -> `Account`.

## Step 3: Choose Account or Bring Your Own Keys

You have two paths:

- Sign in with an AgentOne account
- Select `Or continue without an account`

### If you sign in

Signing in enables account-based features such as:

- Access to AgentOne models
- Settings sync across devices
- Billing and plan management from the app

### If you continue without an account

AgentOne takes you directly to provider setup so you can enable your own providers and add API keys.

In this mode, your API keys stay on your device.

## Step 4: Finish Setup

If you chose the bring-your-own-key path, the onboarding screen shows the same provider controls used later in `Settings` -> `Providers`.

You can finish setup after at least one provider is enabled.

## Step 5: Welcome Screen

The final screen includes these actions:

- `Launch AgentOne`
- `Browse Documentation`
- `Take the Tutorial` (shown as coming soon)

After that, AgentOne opens the main chat screen.

## Good To Know

- Onboarding only appears until you complete it
- Your name can be updated later
- You can sign in later from `Settings` -> `Account`
- You can change providers at any time from `Settings` -> `Providers`
