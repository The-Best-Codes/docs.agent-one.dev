---
title: Sign In and Plan
description: Sign in to AgentOne, view your current plan, and manage billing from the app.
---

When you sign in, AgentOne can show account-based features in the `Plan & Usage` card.

## What You Can See

- your current plan name
- renewal date for an active subscription
- credit usage progress
- remaining credits for the current period

## Account Actions

Depending on your account state, AgentOne can show:

- `Upgrade your Plan`
- `Manage Billing`
- `Account Dashboard`

## If You Are Not Signed In

The app shows a free-state message and prompts you to sign in to load subscription details.

## Hide AgentOne Models

In the `Sync & Access` card, signed-in users can toggle `Hide AgentOne models` to remove AgentOne-hosted models from the model selector.

This setting is only useful if you are signed in and eligible to see those models in the first place.
