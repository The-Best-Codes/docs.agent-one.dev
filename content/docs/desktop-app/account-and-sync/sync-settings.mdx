---
title: Sync Settings
description: Enable settings sync and understand what AgentOne sync currently covers.
---

Signed-in users can enable `Synchronize my settings` from `Settings` -> `Account`.

## What Sync Does

Today, sync is for settings.

That means AgentOne can keep your app preferences in sync across devices when you are signed in.

## What Sync Does Not Mean

The broader product messaging notes that settings sync is available, while synchronization for chats and encrypted API keys is planned separately.

## Requirement

You must be signed in to enable sync.

If you are not signed in, the toggle stays disabled.
