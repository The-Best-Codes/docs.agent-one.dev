---
title: Exports and Deletion
description: Export single chats or multiple chats, and delete chats safely.
---

AgentOne gives you both single-chat and bulk management actions.

## Export A Single Chat

1. Open the chat menu.
2. Click `Export Chat`.
3. Choose `Download as JSON`.

AgentOne prompts you to choose where to save the exported file.

## Bulk Export

1. Enter selection mode.
2. Select one or more chats.
3. Click the bulk export button.
4. Save the combined JSON export file.

## Delete A Single Chat

1. Open the chat menu.
2. Click `Delete Chat`.
3. Confirm deletion.

If you delete the currently open chat, AgentOne returns you to a blank chat view.

## Bulk Delete

1. Enter selection mode.
2. Select the chats you want to remove.
3. Click the bulk delete button.
4. Confirm deletion.

<Callout title="Deletion is permanent" type="warning">
  Chat deletion cannot be undone from the app.
</Callout>
