Open `Settings` from the bottom of the sidebar.

The settings view contains these sections:

- `Account`
- `Appearance`
- `Chats`
- `Extensions`
- `Keyboard Shortcuts`
- `Performance`
- `Providers`
- `Help & Updates`

This part of the docs focuses on the settings that affect the app experience directly.
