---
title: Dangling Extensions
description: Find and clean up extensions that no longer match known registry entries.
---

The Extensions page includes a `Find dangling extensions` action in the filter menu.

Use this when you suspect you have old or mismatched registry-installed extensions that are no longer represented cleanly.

## What You Can Do

- inspect dangling extension entries
- remove one at a time
- remove all dangling entries at once

This is mainly a cleanup feature for keeping your extension list tidy.
