AgentOne is free. Paid plans give you more monthly credits for AgentOne's own model, plus early access to new features.

![The AgentOne pricing page comparing the Free, Pro, Ultra, and Hyper plans.](/images/docs/account/pricing.png)

## Plans

| Plan      | Price         | Monthly credits | Highlights                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | ------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**  | $0            | 100             | Settings sync across devices, all extensions and core features                                                                                                                        |
| **Pro**   | $8.99/month   | 1,000           | Everything in Free, plus chat sync and 10 GB of account data (beta), smarter models, early access to new features, and encrypted API key sync (beta). Starts with a 3-day free trial. |
| **Ultra** | $29.99/month  | 5,000           | Everything in Pro, plus 50 GB of storage (beta), instant access to the smartest models (beta), cloud agent runs (beta), and priority support                                          |
| **Hyper** | $239.99/month | 75,000          | Everything in Ultra, plus 1 TB of storage, cloud agents, and API access (beta)                                                                                                        |

Features marked beta are being rolled out in stages, so they may not be available on every account yet. For the latest details, see the [pricing page](https://www.agent-one.dev/pricing).

## How Credits Work

- Credits are only used when you chat with **AgentOne's own model** (shown as **Default** under **AgentOne** in the model selector).
- Each message costs credits based on how much work the model does, so short questions cost less than long tasks with many tool calls.
- Credits **refresh every month** on your plan's renewal date.
- Using [your own API keys](/docs/models/built-in-providers) or [local models](/docs/models/local-models) never uses credits. You pay your provider directly, with no markup from AgentOne.

## Check Your Credits

Open `Settings` -> `Account`. **Monthly credits used** shows a progress bar and how many credits are left before they reset.

![The Sync & Access card showing 26% of monthly credits used, with 742 credits remaining.](/images/docs/settings/account.png)

The web dashboard at [agent-one.dev/dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) shows the same information.

## Running Out of Credits

When you run out, AgentOne's model stops responding until your credits reset. You can:

- **Upgrade** your plan from `Settings` -> `Account` or the account menu.
- Switch the chat to a model from [your own provider](/docs/models) in the model selector.

## Change or Cancel Your Plan

Open the account menu and click **Upgrade Your Plan** or **Manage Billing**. This opens the **Billing** page in your web dashboard.

- **Change plans.** If you already have a paid plan, click **Upgrade to** or **Switch to** under another plan and confirm. Your plan changes right away. When you upgrade, the card on file is charged the prorated difference for the rest of the billing period. When you switch to a cheaper plan, your billing is adjusted for the rest of the period.
- **Update payment, see invoices, or cancel.** Click **Modify or Cancel** to open the billing portal.

If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, and then your account moves to the Free plan.
