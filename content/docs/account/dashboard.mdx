title: "Use the website dashboard"
description: "Find account settings, billing, sessions, and integration status."
---

Open the [AgentOne dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) and sign in to the same account you use in the desktop app.

## Overview

Overview summarizes the account, plan, and available usage information. Check the displayed account before diagnosing a missing subscription or settings mismatch.

## Billing

Use **Billing** to inspect usage, review upgrades, or open subscription management. Follow the current portal's controls to manage the subscription, and verify the resulting plan and billing status. See [credits and plans](/docs/account/credits-and-plans).

## Sessions

**Sessions** shows account session information and revocation controls. Review the device and timing before revoking a session. Revocation can require signing in again on that device.

## Settings

The website's **Settings** includes display-name changes, email changes, password controls, and linked sign-in methods. An email change requires verification before the address is changed. Use the recovery flow if you don't know the current password.

These website account controls are separate from desktop preferences. Appearance, providers, tool permissions, AI Instructions, and other app behavior are configured in AgentOne.

## Integrations

The dashboard's official integrations area may show upcoming features. For tools available in the current desktop app, use **Browse Extensions**. A website placeholder doesn't mean installed desktop extensions are unavailable.

For sign-in problems, start with [Sign in to AgentOne](/docs/account/sign-in). For sync expectations, see [data and sync](/docs/account/data-and-sync).
