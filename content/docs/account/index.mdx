You can use AgentOne without an account by connecting [your own AI provider](/docs/models). An AgentOne account adds:

- **AgentOne's own model**, which works right away with free monthly credits. No API keys needed.
- **Settings sync** across all your computers.
- **Paid plans** with more credits and early access to new features. See [Plans & Credits](/docs/account/plans-and-credits).

## Sign In

You can sign in during [onboarding](/docs/quickstart), or any time later:

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Steps>
<Step>

    ### Start signing in

    Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and click **Sign In**. This takes you to `Settings` -> `Account`. Click **Sign in**.

</Step>
<Step>

    ### Approve the sign-in in your browser

    AgentOne opens a sign-in page in your web browser and shows a code under **Link your account**.

    ![AgentOne showing Link your account with an eight-character code and an Open Login Form Manually link.](/images/docs/quickstart/onboarding-link-account.png)

    1. Check that the code in your browser matches this code.
    2. If your browser didn't open, click **Open Login Form Manually**.

    Sign in on the web page (or create an account with email, Google, GitHub, or Discord), then click **Approve**.

</Step>
<Step>

    ### You're signed in

    AgentOne notices the approval and finishes signing in by itself. Your name and plan appear in the account menu, and AgentOne's model appears in the [model selector](/docs/models/choosing-a-model).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Brand-new account?">
  Right after you sign up, AgentOne may show **Account setup in progress**. Your
  credits usually appear within a few minutes, and chat starts working as soon
  as they do.
</Callout>

## Your Account in the App

Your account details live in `Settings` -> `Account` -> **Sync & Access**.

![The Sync & Access card showing the signed-in user, plan, monthly credits used, and sync switches.](/images/docs/settings/account.png)

From here you can:

- Open the **Account Dashboard** on the web.
- See your plan, when it renews, and how many **Monthly credits used** you have left.
- **Upgrade** or **Manage Billing**.
- Turn **Synchronize my settings** on or off. See [Sync & Privacy](/docs/account/sync-and-privacy).
- Turn on **Hide AgentOne models** if you only want to use your own providers.
- **Sign out**.

## The Web Dashboard

Your account dashboard at [agent-one.dev/dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) has a few more options:

| Page         | What it's for                                                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Overview** | Your credit usage for the month and when it resets.                                        |
| **Billing**  | Change your plan, start a trial, or open the billing portal for invoices.                  |
| **Sessions** | See every device signed in to your account and revoke any you don't recognize.             |
| **Settings** | Change your display name, email, or password, and link Google, GitHub, or Discord sign-in. |

## Sign Out

Open the account menu and click **Sign Out**, or click **Sign out** in `Settings` -> `Account`. AgentOne asks you to confirm, because your settings stop syncing until you sign in again. Your chats stay on your computer.

You can also sign a device out remotely from **Sessions** in the web dashboard.
