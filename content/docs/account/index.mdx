title: "Your AgentOne account"
description: "Sign in, check usage, and understand the desktop and website settings."
---

An AgentOne account connects the desktop app to AgentOne's built-in model and settings sync. Your own model providers and local servers have separate configuration and access requirements.

Open **Configure AgentOne → Account** for desktop sign-in, usage, settings sync, your name, AI Instructions, and Memory. Open the [website dashboard](https://www.agent-one.dev/dashboard) for website account details and billing.
