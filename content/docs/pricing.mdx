AgentOne offers three plans to fit your needs.

## Plans

### Free — $0/month

- Free account
- Access to AgentOne models
- Core agent features and extensions
- Limited daily usage

### Pro — $8.99/month

- Much higher usage limits
- Sync across devices
- Priority access to new features
- Everything in Free

### Ultra — $29.99/month

- Highest usage limits
- Best-performance model tier
- Advanced workflow support
- Everything in Pro

## Bring Your Own Key

You can also use your own API keys to access providers directly, independent of any plan. See [AI Providers](/docs/providers) for details on configuring your own keys.
