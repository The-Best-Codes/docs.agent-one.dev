Extensions connect AgentOne to external apps and services. Once linked, AgentOne can act inside these apps on your behalf — reading data, creating content, sending messages, and more.

## Available Extensions

AgentOne offers 2,000+ extensions across a wide range of categories:

### Communication

Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams

### Productivity

Notion, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Jira, Canva

### Social

Reddit, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Spotify

### Developer

GitHub, GitLab, Docker, Kubernetes, Vercel, NPM, Python

### Cloud & Infrastructure

AWS, Azure, GCP, Cloudflare, MongoDB, PostgreSQL

## How Extensions Work

Extensions are powered by [MCP (Model Context Protocol)](/docs/mcp) servers. Each extension exposes a set of tools that AgentOne can call during task execution.

You can also add your own custom MCP servers to extend AgentOne with any functionality you need. See the [MCP Servers](/docs/mcp) page for details.
