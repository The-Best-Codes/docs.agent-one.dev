---
title: "Manage scheduled agents"
description: "Search, edit, pause, resume, and delete recurring tasks."
---

Open **Scheduled Agents** in the sidebar. Search matches the title, cron schedule, and prompt, so you can find a task even if you only remember one of its sources.

## Edit a task

Expand its entry, change the title, schedule, or prompt, then choose **Save**. **Cancel** discards those edits. Check the readable schedule after saving and inspect the next result to verify the changed prompt.

Changing the saved task affects future runs. Earlier chats remain as records of what happened at those times.

## Pause and resume

Turn the entry's switch off to disable future scheduled launches. The title, prompt, and schedule remain saved. Turn it back on to resume scheduling.

Pausing a schedule doesn't stop a response that's already running. Stop that response from its chat if needed.

## Delete

Use **Delete** and review the confirmation. Deleting removes the schedule and stops future runs. Chats created by earlier runs remain; manage those separately through [chat deletion](/docs/chat/export-and-delete).

## Manage through chat

With the scheduled-agent tools enabled, you can ask AgentOne to list tasks, change a prompt or schedule, pause or resume a task, or delete it. Modification tools require approval by default. Identify the intended task clearly and verify the result on the Scheduled Agents page.

## Another computer

Schedules don't migrate through settings sync. Create and test the task separately on the device that should run it. Avoid creating duplicate enabled schedules on several machines unless you want several independent runs.
