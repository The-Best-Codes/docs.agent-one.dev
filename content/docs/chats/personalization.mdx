title: Personalization
description: Tell AgentOne your name, give it standing instructions and instruction files, and manage what it remembers about you.
---

AgentOne can tailor every chat to you. Your name, custom instructions, instruction files, and memories are included in every conversation. Your name, instructions, and memories [sync](/docs/account/sync-and-privacy) across your devices when you're signed in.

Find them in `Settings` -> `Account` -> **Profile & Instructions**.

![The Profile & Instructions card with Your Name, AI Instructions, Instruction files, Skills, and three saved memories.](/images/docs/settings/profile.png)

## Your Name

AgentOne uses this name to address you, including in the greeting on new chats. If you signed in during onboarding, it's filled in from your account.

## AI Instructions

Standing instructions that AgentOne follows in every chat, up to 2,000 characters. For example:

```text
Keep answers short unless I ask for detail. Use metric units.
I'm a product designer, so skip the basics on UX terminology.
```

[Projects](/docs/chats/projects) can add their own instructions on top of these.

## Instruction Files

Instruction files are Markdown files with instructions AgentOne reads at the start of every chat. If you already keep an `AGENTS.md` or `CLAUDE.md` for other coding agents, AgentOne can follow it too.

By default, AgentOne reads `~/AGENTS.md` if it exists. Under **Instruction files**, click **Add** to choose more files, or the **x** next to a file to stop using it. The file itself isn't deleted.

- Files that don't exist are skipped, so it's fine to list a file you haven't created yet.
- If files disagree, files later in the list take priority.
- [Projects](/docs/chats/projects) with a folder also read the `AGENTS.md` in that folder, and you can choose each project's files in its settings.

Instruction files and [skill](/docs/agent/skills) folders are saved on each computer and don't sync between devices.

## Memory

Memories are short facts AgentOne remembers about you across chats, like your preferences, goals, or ongoing projects.

There are two ways to add them:

- **Ask AgentOne.** Say something like "Remember that I prefer short answers." AgentOne saves it with its built-in **Memory** tool.
- **Add them yourself.** Click **Add** in the **Memory** section and type a short, specific fact.

To change a memory, edit its text. To remove one, click the **x** next to it and confirm. You can save up to 100 memories of up to 500 characters each.

<Callout title="Keep memories short and specific">
  "Lives in Portland, Oregon" works better than a paragraph about your life.
  AgentOne reuses short facts more reliably.
</Callout>

If you don't want AgentOne to save memories on its own, turn off **Allow AgentOne to edit memories** in the same card. You can still add memories yourself.

[Projects](/docs/chats/projects) with **Separate memory** turned on keep their own memory instead of using these.
