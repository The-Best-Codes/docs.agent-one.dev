---
title: Message Actions
description: Copy, edit, branch, regenerate, and read messages aloud in AgentOne.
---

Every message has a row of small buttons underneath it. By default they're always visible. You can show them only on hover, or hide them, with `Settings` -> `Chats` -> **Message Action Row**.

## Actions on AgentOne's Responses

![The action buttons under a response: copy, branch, and regenerate.](/images/docs/chats/message-actions.png)

1. **Copy** copies the response's text.
2. **Branch** starts a new chat from this point. See [Branch a Conversation](#branch-a-conversation).
3. **Regenerate** asks AgentOne to write this response again.

If you've set up [text-to-speech](/docs/models/text-to-speech), a **Read aloud** button also appears. Click it again to stop reading.

## Actions on Your Messages

![The action buttons under your message: copy and edit.](/images/docs/chats/user-message-actions.png)

1. **Copy** copies your message.
2. **Edit** lets you change your message.

## Edit a Message

Click **Edit** under one of your messages to change it in place.

![Editing a message, with Cancel, Save and restart from here, and a menu arrow next to it.](/images/docs/chats/edit-message.png)

1. **Save and restart from here** saves your change and has AgentOne answer again from this point. Everything after the message is replaced.
2. The arrow menu has **Regenerate when saved**. Turn it off to save your edit without getting a new answer. The button then just says **Save**.

Press `Enter` to save or `Esc` to cancel. To change the default, use `Settings` -> `Chats` -> **Regenerate on Save**.

<Callout>
  You can edit your own messages, but not AgentOne's responses. To get a
  different response, use **Regenerate** or **Branch**.
</Callout>

## Branch a Conversation

Branching lets you try a different direction without losing the original chat. Click **Branch** under any of AgentOne's responses. AgentOne makes a new chat with every message up to and including that response, using the same title, model, and settings, and opens it.

The new chat appears in the sidebar with a split-arrow icon. Hover it to see which chat it came from.

![A branched chat in the sidebar with the tooltip Branched from Weekend in Lisbon itinerary.](/images/docs/chats/branch-indicator.png)

## Resume a Response

If a response was cut off by an error, click **Resume** above the message box. AgentOne continues where it left off instead of starting over. See [When Something Goes Wrong](/docs/chats#when-something-goes-wrong).
