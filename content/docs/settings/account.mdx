`Settings` -> `Account` has two cards.

## Sync & Access

![The Sync & Access card with the signed-in user, the Pro plan, monthly credits used, and switches for sync, hiding AgentOne models, and settings changes.](/images/docs/settings/account.png)

| Setting                               | What it does                                                                                                                                       | Default |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Account                               | Sign in or out, and open the **Account Dashboard**. See [Your AgentOne Account](/docs/account).                                                    |         |
| Plan                                  | Your plan, its renewal date, and **Upgrade** or **Manage Billing**. See [Plans & Credits](/docs/account/plans-and-credits).                        |         |
| **Monthly credits used**              | How many of your monthly credits you've used, and when they reset.                                                                                 |         |
| **Synchronize my settings**           | Keeps your settings in sync across devices. See [Sync & Privacy](/docs/account/sync-and-privacy).                                                  | On      |
| **Hide AgentOne models**              | Removes AgentOne's model from the model selector.                                                                                                  | Off     |
| **Allow AgentOne to change settings** | Lets AgentOne look up and change your settings when you ask. See [Tools & Approvals](/docs/agent/tools-and-approvals#changing-settings-by-asking). | On      |
| **Ask before changing settings**      | Asks before each settings change, unless [Auto mode](/docs/agent/auto-mode) approves it.                                                           | On      |

**Synchronize my settings** and **Hide AgentOne models** need you to be signed in, and neither one syncs between devices.

## Profile & Instructions

![The Profile & Instructions card with Your Name, AI Instructions, Instruction files, Skills, and Memory.](/images/docs/settings/profile.png)

| Setting                               | What it does                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your Name**                         | The name AgentOne uses to address you.                                                                                              |
| **AI Instructions**                   | Instructions AgentOne follows in every chat, up to 2,000 characters.                                                                |
| **Instruction files**                 | Files like `AGENTS.md` that AgentOne follows in every chat. See [Instruction Files](/docs/chats/personalization#instruction-files). |
| **Skills**                            | Folders AgentOne looks in for [skills](/docs/agent/skills).                                                                         |
| **Memory**                            | Facts AgentOne remembers about you across chats. Click **Add** to add one, or the **x** to delete.                                  |
| **Allow AgentOne to edit memories**   | Lets AgentOne add, change, and remove memories on its own as you chat.                                                              |
| **Allow AgentOne to read past chats** | Lets AgentOne search and read your earlier chats. See [Chat History](/docs/agent/chat-history).                                     |

See [Personalization](/docs/chats/personalization) for tips.
