`Settings` -> `Account` has two cards.

## Sync & Access

![The Sync & Access card with the signed-in user, the Pro plan, monthly credits used, and two switches.](/images/docs/settings/account.png)

| Setting                     | What it does                                                                                                                | Default |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Account                     | Sign in or out, and open the **Account Dashboard**. See [Your AgentOne Account](/docs/account).                             |         |
| Plan                        | Your plan, its renewal date, and **Upgrade** or **Manage Billing**. See [Plans & Credits](/docs/account/plans-and-credits). |         |
| **Monthly credits used**    | How many of your monthly credits you've used, and when they reset.                                                          |         |
| **Synchronize my settings** | Keeps your settings in sync across devices. See [Sync & Privacy](/docs/account/sync-and-privacy).                           | On      |
| **Hide AgentOne models**    | Removes AgentOne's model from the model selector.                                                                           | Off     |

Both switches need you to be signed in, and neither one syncs between devices.

## Profile & Instructions

![The Profile & Instructions card with Your Name, AI Instructions, and Memory.](/images/docs/settings/profile.png)

| Setting             | What it does                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your Name**       | The name AgentOne uses to address you.                                                             |
| **AI Instructions** | Instructions AgentOne follows in every chat, up to 2,000 characters.                               |
| **Memory**          | Facts AgentOne remembers about you across chats. Click **Add** to add one, or the **x** to delete. |

See [Personalization](/docs/chats/personalization) for tips.
