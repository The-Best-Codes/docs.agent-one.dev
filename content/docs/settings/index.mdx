Open settings from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or press `Cmd + ,` on macOS or `Ctrl + ,` on Windows and Linux.

![The settings page with the Back to Chat button, Search settings, and the On this page sidebar.](/images/docs/settings/overview.png)

1. **Back to Chat** returns you to the chat you were in.
2. **Search settings** finds any setting by name.
3. The sidebar lists every section and card. It follows along as you scroll, and you can click any entry to jump to it.

Settings is one long page, so you can also just scroll through everything.

## Search Settings

Press `Cmd + K` (macOS) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows and Linux) on the settings page, or click **Search settings**. Search understands related words, so "dark mode," "night mode," and "color scheme" all find **Theme**. Choose a result to scroll to it. The setting briefly highlights so you can spot it.

![The settings search window with theme typed in and the Theme setting as the top result.](/images/docs/settings/search.png)

## Reset a Setting

Most settings have a reset button on the right. Click it to go back to the default value.

## Let AgentOne Change Settings for You

You can ask AgentOne to change many settings, like "Switch to dark mode" or "Make the text larger." It uses its built-in settings tools and asks for your approval before changing anything (or lets [Auto mode](/docs/agent/auto-mode) decide). Turn this off with **Allow AgentOne to change settings** in `Settings` -> `Account`. Private settings, like your API keys and account, can't be read or changed this way. See [Tools & Approvals](/docs/agent/tools-and-approvals).
